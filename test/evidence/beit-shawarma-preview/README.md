# A diner browses the Beit Shawarma preview — evidence

> **Feature:** `PROFILE-010` · **Scenario:** `verify/scenarios/beit-shawarma-preview.yml` · **Generated by:** `node verify/runner.mjs --only beit-shawarma-preview` against a production build on 2026-11-24

A diner opens `/beit-shawarma/`, sees the preview banner and the restaurant's headline, follows "See the menu" to every dish with its price, and reaches the contact page's message form. The old site's `/home` address redirects to the new home page.

## How it was run

The site was built with the production config (`LANG=C.UTF-8 bundle exec jekyll build`: `github-pages` with Jekyll 3.10 and the remote theme bamr87/zer0-mistakes at its default branch) and served with `python3 -m http.server`. The scenario ran at 390×844 and 1366×768 and passed at both.

- Jekyll copied `beit-shawarma/` byte for byte (`diff -rq` against the build output found no difference), left `src/` out of `_site`, and kept the preview out of `sitemap.xml`.
- Every page carries `robots: noindex,nofollow`, and the canonical URL is the preview's own address (`https://bamr87.github.io/beit-shawarma/`), never www.beitshawarma.com. The home page's structured data parses as a schema.org `Restaurant`.
- The menu shows 33 dish cards and a 6-drink price list: 39 prices, every one in `$0.00` form.
- `/beit-shawarma/home/` lands on `/beit-shawarma/`.
- No horizontal scroll and no console errors at either size. htmlproofer (the `link-check.yml` command) passed on the whole build: 1,137 internal links in 155 files.

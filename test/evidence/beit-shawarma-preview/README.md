# A diner browses the Beit Shawarma preview — evidence

> **Feature:** `PROFILE-010` · **Scenario:** `verify/scenarios/beit-shawarma-preview.yml` · **Generated by:** `node verify/runner.mjs --only beit-shawarma-preview` against a production build on 2026-11-24

A diner opens `/beit-shawarma/`, sees the preview notice in the announcement bar and the restaurant's headline, follows "See the menu" to the menu cards, switches to the Party trays tab for the catering page and its request form, and reaches the contact page. The old site's `/home` address redirects to the new home page.

## How it was run

The site was built with the production config (`LANG=C.UTF-8 bundle exec jekyll build`: `github-pages` with Jekyll 3.10 and the remote theme bamr87/zer0-mistakes at its default branch) and served with `python3 -m http.server`. The scenario ran at 390×844 and 1366×768 and passed at both.

- **Jekyll output:** `beit-shawarma/` came through byte for byte (`diff -rq` against the build output found no difference). `src/` was left out of `_site`, and the preview stayed out of `sitemap.xml`.
- **Search engines:** every page carries `robots: noindex,nofollow`, and the canonical URL is the preview's own address (`https://bamr87.github.io/beit-shawarma/`), never www.beitshawarma.com. The home page's structured data parses as a schema.org `Restaurant`.
- **Prices:** the menu shows 37 priced items in 6 cream cards, and the catering page the other 2 (Party Tray 1 at $175.99 and Party Tray 2 at $90.99). That is all 39 items in `content/menu.json`, every price in `$0.00` form.
- **Redirect:** `/beit-shawarma/home/` lands on `/beit-shawarma/`.
- **Layout and links:** no horizontal scroll and no console errors at either size. htmlproofer, with the `link-check.yml` command, passed on the whole build: 1,139 internal links in 156 files.
- **Accessibility:** a separate axe-core WCAG 2.1 AA and best-practice pass over home, menu, catering, about and contact found 0 violations at both sizes.

About us

Beit means home

Beit is Arabic for home, and “Tastes like home” is the standard we cook to.

Welcome to Beit Shawarma! We specialize in authentic Middle Eastern cuisine, serving freshly prepared shawarma, falafel, salads, and more. Our mission is to bring delicious flavors with high-quality ingredients in a cozy and friendly atmosphere.

Whether you are craving a quick snack, a hearty meal, or catering for your event, we make sure every bite is a delight.

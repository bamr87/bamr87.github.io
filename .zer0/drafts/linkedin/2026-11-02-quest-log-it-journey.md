Corporate training ends in a certificate nobody reads. My curriculum publishes its own report card instead. It reads 0 of 27 perfect.

That is IT-Journey, Level 0000 of my Quest Log: a free curriculum whose levels run from 0000 to 1111.

All summer, AI agents took the course. They ran the commands in a sandbox and wrote down what broke. One quest sent learners to a folder that never existed. Another collapsed most of its page into one unreadable block. A fix only stuck if a score held or went up, never because the model graded its own homework.

Then the loop went quiet on 9 September, and the scoreboard has said 0 of 27 ever since. I publish it anyway. A training department would put that number in an appendix.

Day 1 of my daily deep dive gave the repo 72 out of 100. Two of its bugs were fixed the next day. Six issues now wait on a human, because the robots' own rules escalated them.

The screenshots and the full list are in the article.
